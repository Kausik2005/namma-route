    Your week on this trip

    Every cell is the road trip time for that day and departure time, coloured against the quickest one. Tap a cell to plan for it. Useful for picking office days and hours.

    Places: every junction plus about 180 localities and landmarks and every metro station. Each one joins the road network at its nearest junction, with a short stretch on local roads at either end. Roads: a simplified network of about 110 junctions, from Nelamangala and Bidadi to Hoskote and Attibele, and the arterial roads between them (ORR, NICE Road, Hosur Rd, Bellary Rd, Old Madras Rd, Tumkur Rd, Mysore Rd, Sarjapur Rd and others). Positions are approximate and road lengths are estimated.

    Traffic: a typical-day model, not live data. It has morning and evening peaks, tidal flow toward the tech corridors in the morning and away in the evening, lighter weekends and the known bottlenecks.

    Costs are rough guides: petrol at ₹103 a litre, 14 km/l for a car and 45 km/l for a two-wheeler, Electronic City Elevated Expressway toll about ₹70 (₹30 on two wheels), NH 44 airport toll about ₹125 for cars, NICE Road about ₹90 (₹35 on two wheels), other toll plazas about ₹70 (₹30 on two wheels), autos at ₹36 for the first 2 km then ₹18 a km, and BMRCL distance fares from ₹10 to ₹90.

    Metro: the Purple, Green and Yellow lines, plus the Pink and Blue lines when you include lines being built, with approximate station positions, typical frequencies and service from about 05:00 to 23:00. The first and last mile is a walk under 1.3 km, otherwise an auto or your two-wheeler through traffic. Check BMRCL for exact timings.
